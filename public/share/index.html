01 — Brand essence

A community that became a brand.

BIG CRUISE is a Nigerian-leaning X/Twitter community. Not a startup, transport company, yacht club, or tourism board. Organic online family: cruising, good vibes, real talk, loyalty, roasts, music, Spaces, jobs, birthdays, showing up. 18+.

Founder: FX @13fxiii. Community: @BCHub_. Tag: #FXIII. Former names Cruise Connect Hub / Cruise & Connect Hub are retired and must never appear as current identity.

Official line: Where the cruise lives. Philosophy: Cruise. Connect. Create. Support. Grow. Feeling: These are my people. Nigeria ↔ diaspora.

EXTERNAL AI REVIEW — BRAND DESIGN DESCRIPTION

Source of truth

What BIG CRUISE is

A Nigerian X/Twitter community that became a cultural brand. Cruising, banter, loyalty, music, Spaces, jobs, birthdays, adult conversation, diaspora connection. Founder FX @13fxiii, community @BCHub_, tag #FXIII.

What the 〽️ represents

The mark members already type. Community signature, not a decorative emoji and not a new mascot. Geometry: lopsided M / rollercoaster, one stroke, two peaks, dip, drop right. Path above. Live mark = open ring + 〽️. Midnight mark = filled circle + 〽️.

Why Danfo colours

Lagos night + the yellow of the city's danfo. Colour identity, not a bus drawing. Midnight dominates. Yellow hits.

What was rejected

Yacht / boat / anchor concepts. Literal danfo bus as logo. Gold, neon, chrome, glow. Generic African tourism (masks, safari, Adinkra/Ankara clichés, palm-tree luxury). Children's palettes. Smiley/chat-bubble systems. Stacked-chevron “spark”. Former names as current branding. Google Font “Danfo” as display type.

How 7 Days works

Seven weekly sub-brands live inside the house. Each has a palette, motif, voice, programs, merch capsule, social skin. Master lockups stay black + #F5C400. Tuesday Electric Yellow #FFF200 is a club hit, not a new house yellow.

How 49 sub-themes work

7×7. Each has a distinct job (merch print, post, Space, game). Listed in section 05. Full descriptions live in the brand book and BRAND.md.

Merch

Streetwear capsule. Symbols, 〽️, sub-brand names, numbers, motifs. No weekday slogans as filler type. No former name.

Social

Fourteen template jobs, four formats, seven colourways. Chat cards are square culture stickers, not event posters.

How the master connects the week

Every day still sits on midnight. Every day still carries the 〽️. Day motifs are supporting graphics. If a piece does not feel like BIG CRUISE, it is rejected.

18+

Attitude, crop, nightlife, redaction, humour. Suggestive editorial, not graphic sexual imagery.

Kept line: Where the cruise lives. Philosophy: Cruise. Connect. Create. Support. Grow.

App environment

CruiseBackground is the global night of the product. Midnight 85–90%, Danfo Yellow 10–15%, tiled vector mark (never emoji stickers), -8 degree lanes, grain, slow motion paths. Densities: cover, default, quiet, game (a 7 Days accent wash so UNO is not Ludo). Chrome: CruiseButton, Card, Modal, Badge, Avatar, Header, GameRoom, PlayerCard, Notification, Loader (1.2s skippable boot), Toast.

Game Room + ID

One community, many game worlds. Lobby: games, active rooms, recents, challenges. Architected for private rooms, invites, tournaments, spectating, history. BIG CRUISE ID is local (BCH-XXXXXX, level, points, badges, stats). Auth off. BCH points are a ledger, not a shop.
